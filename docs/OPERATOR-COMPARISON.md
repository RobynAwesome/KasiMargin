# Proposed consented operator comparison

Status: prepared experiment; recruitment, consent and execution are pending. Real technical inference does not establish comprehension benefit.

## Question and participant

Can the approved local-model selection help one willing micro-enterprise operator understand profit versus available cash more accurately or quickly than the deterministic baseline? The proposed first context is an owner-operated spaza shop considering restock. The owner chooses a suitable consenting participant; the agent does not send invitations or contact anyone.

Explain the prototype, purpose, approximate duration, what will be recorded, and the ability to stop without consequence before obtaining consent. Use an anonymous participant code. Do not publish names, contact details, live balances or receipts. Save consent and observations only in ignored `local-data/operator-comparison/`. Use synthetic data first; real records require separate explicit permission and remain private.

## Materials and procedure

1. Confirm the operator's comfortable language and device. The current trusted renderer is English; if that does not fit, revise and validate the language material before testing.
2. Record the exact code commit, model tag/digest and admitted selections used. Reject/fallback outputs must remain labelled and are not a model condition.
3. Use matched synthetic cases covering positive cash, credit sales, missing obligations and a deficit. Keep financial facts identical between explanation conditions; only the permitted fact order/options may differ.
4. Present the deterministic baseline and admitted model explanation in alternating order across cases. Record order and prior exposure; repeated exposure can teach the answer. One operator is a feasibility check, not a statistically reliable benefit estimate.
5. Without coaching, ask the operator to explain the position and answer the prompts below. Record the answer, time and requests for help before any teaching or correction.
6. Debrief, clarify the distinction, and let the operator correct misunderstandings. Record correction time separately from the unaided attempt.

## Comprehension prompts

| Prompt | Expected understanding |
|---|---|
| Which amount is estimated profit, and which is conditional cash headroom? | Distinguish performance from liquidity |
| Can the unpaid customer sale be spent now? | Uncollected credit is excluded from cash |
| Why is this case unknown rather than zero? | Missing records prevent a cash assessment |
| Which obligations and buffer must be protected? | Identify amounts included within the horizon |
| Does this result approve buying stock? | The output is conditional and does not authorize a purchase |

Score each prompt as correct, partly correct, incorrect or unable to answer, preserving the actual response privately. Record errors, assistance, completion time and confidence separately. A faster answer with a new misunderstanding is not a successful result.

## Cost, usefulness and limits

Record setup/supervision time, inference latency, compute/resource measurement method and recovery from failures. Ask whether the operator would return, what was confusing, and what change would make the tool useful. Willingness to pay is testimony; it is distinct from a purchase or attributable economic outcome.

The selection-only AI may add little over the baseline. If comprehension is unchanged or worse, preserve that finding and reconsider the task design without weakening arithmetic, unknown-state handling or output validation. Do not promote technical admission to human benefit, economic value, teacher approval or deployment readiness.
