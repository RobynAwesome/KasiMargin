Help me understand KasiMargin

1 · Bring your figures

Use your sales record, cash count and unpaid bills. No sign-up or bank connection is needed in this local prototype.

2 · See two different pictures

Margin is sales less their costs. Cash headroom is what remains after recorded bills, other promises and your reserve. A sale on credit is not cash in hand.

3 · Check what needs attention

Unknown means something still needs checking. It does not mean zero or that you have done something wrong. Review the records before making your decision.

4 · Keep a copy if you choose

Changes stay in this page until you save or export a result. Closing or reloading can lose your draft. Saved receipts go to this computer; exports go to your browser's downloads.

The calculation works without AI. The optional local assistant can choose explanation points; it cannot change the amounts. “How this was checked” shows the source and checks behind a result.
